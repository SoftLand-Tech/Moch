Theme mockup — Mocheme vs Relay

Click the menu icon in either phone to open its themed sidebar (close icon or scrim to dismiss). Relay keeps today's flat component design with the old cyan/teal palette. Mocheme restructures the components: floating tray header, assistant replies in soft blob cards with a Moch avatar, cream user bubbles with a tail, dashed thinking pill with blush dots, rounder composer with a gradient send button, pill chips, and a drawer with a rounded cap, icon chips and a gradient New chat. All icons are the app's real Ionicons.
